01 / SYSTEMATIC DECISIONS

A visible path from question to scope.

Five bounded preparation checkpoints bring structure to technical discussions without pretending the work has already been implemented.

01 — DISCOVER◎

Understand the problem.

Record the challenge, source, domain, commercial constraints and qualification decision.

02 — SPECIFY▤

Define the boundaries.

Capture dependencies, permissions, deliverables and acceptance expectations.

03 — IMPLEMENT◇

Plan implementation.

Compare scoped options, assumptions, trade-offs and indicative estimates.

04 — VERIFY⌁

Trace acceptance.

Link criteria to manually recorded evidence plans, gaps and blocking issues.

05 — HAND OVER⬡

Prepare an internal draft.

Summarise the selected scope and its limitations before any external step.
